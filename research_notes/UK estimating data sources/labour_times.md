## 1. Thatcham Research: what its methods and times are, and whether they can be licensed directly

### Takeaway
Thatcham's live UK repair-data product today is **escribe / escribe plus**, an online library of repair *methods*. Its historic job-time product (the "Thatcham Time System", MET) dates from 1993, and Thatcham's own focus has since moved to methods. Thatcham data reaches estimating software through licensing deals with systems houses (Audatex, and historically Glassmatix) and with large licensees (Suncorp, Mitchell). I found no public developer API, no published licence terms and no published price for licensing Thatcham times or paint times into third-party software. I also found nothing about products named "Thatcham Methods Assistant" or a "Thatcham paint materials index".

### Cited Findings
- Thatcham began researching repair times in the early 1970s because insurers wanted a reference that was more accurate than opinion. Times were converted to the British Standard 100 (BS100) rating, which reflects the average rate at which trained technicians work. Vehicles are stripped in a research workshop, and the coded items are totalled to give the overall MET (Methods and Times) job time. — [Paint & Panel, "How Thatcham works" (older article, search snippet only)](https://www.paintandpanel.com.au/article/6D3499B0-3215-11DF-87AD005056B05D57)
- The **Thatcham Time System (TTS)**, released in 1993, held the times for replacing parts and panels and incorporated repair methods. Thatcham's focus later shifted towards methods, which a Thatcham manager described as "fundamentally associated with cost". — [Paint & Panel (older article)](https://www.paintandpanel.com.au/article/6D3499B0-3215-11DF-87AD005056B05D57)
- Thatcham sold its data to systems houses including Audatex and Glassmatix. In 2006, crash repair times supplied by vehicle manufacturers (VMs) and used within Audatex were favoured by more than two-thirds of the UK market, and Audatex was then the sole user of VM crash repair times in the UK. Thatcham said it would withhold its data from times-based systems that failed an audit. (2006, historical.) — [AM Online, "Major shift in estimating" (2006, search snippet)](https://am-online.com/news/2006/5/24/major-shift-in-estimating/11940)
- **escribe / escribe plus:** Thatcham streamlined its range into two products. escribe is updated monthly and holds more than 1.3 million repair methods, both VM methods and methods Thatcham devised. escribe plus adds instant access to VM / OEM methods. — [Thatcham press release on Mynewsdesk](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/improved-online-repair-methods-to-meet-the-needs-of-the-crash-repair-sector-1664294)
- A separate figure appears in Thatcham's escribe 10-year release (around 2023): over 500,000 repair methods and close to two million downloads a year. It conflicts with the 1.3 million figure, probably because the two releases count differently or date from different years. — [Thatcham press release, escribe 10 years](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/moving-with-the-times-thatcham-researchs-escribe-celebrates-10-years-of-providing-vital-intelligence-to-the-repair-industry-3262521)
- The same release says repair times and parts pricing are still available through escribe. This comes from a search summary and should be verified. — [Thatcham escribe 10-year release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/moving-with-the-times-thatcham-researchs-escribe-celebrates-10-years-of-providing-vital-intelligence-to-the-repair-industry-3262521)
- In 2026 Thatcham still describes escribe as "the market's leading repair methods solution", produced in its Repair Technology Centre. — [Thatcham headlamp-repair press release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/thatcham-research-enables-sustainable-headlamp-repair-with-new-guidance-3256352)
- **Methods inside an estimating system:** "Thatcham Integrated Methods" (Thatcham.I.M.) gives escribe customers access to methods from within their estimating system. Audatex's AudaEnterpriseGold was the first system to connect, with links to more than 7,000 methods. — [Post Online, "Thatcham applauds solution success"](https://www.postonline.co.uk/node/1649974); [Collision Week](https://collisionweek.com/?p=20298)
- Thatcham also ran "Methods Direct" for subscribers at no extra cost (older), and a registration-number lookup for repair methods launched in 2015. — [Bodyshop Magazine, "Thatcham unveils Methods Direct"](https://www.bodyshopmag.com/?p=7576); [Fleet News 2015](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/2015/3/25/thatcham-launch-registration-based-repair-method-lookup/55256)
- **Bulk or large licensees:** Suncorp signed a £2m, five-year deal to provide Thatcham repair methods *and times* in Australia and New Zealand, and later became the first "Thatcham Research Global Associate". In North America, Mitchell International partnered with Thatcham, whose ThatchamNet complements Mitchell's OEM data. — [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/australian-automotive-industry-draws-on-uk-repair-expertise-in-deal-with-thatcham-research/); [Collision Week 2018](https://collisionweek.com/2018/12/04/australias-suncorp-thatcham-research-renew-collision-repair-data-agreement/); [Aftermarket News](https://www.aftermarketnews.com/mitchell-international-partners-with-thatcham-to-provide-independently-researched-collision-repair-methodologies-to-the-north-american-market/)
- Thatcham has also licensed other datasets directly to insurers and brokers, for example the Variant Code dataset covering more than 50,000 variants, and a WTW partnership. — [Thatcham Variant Code release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/variant-code-gives-insurers-greater-insight-and-data-granularity-3034765); [WTW 2022](https://www.wtwco.com/en-GB/News/2022/01/wtw-partners-with-thatcham-research-to-help-insurers-adapt-to-new-vehicle-technology-challenge)
- **Repairer pricing (dated):** Thatcham introduced monthly payment plans alongside annual contracts in 2014, and said the new packages could save up to £900 on the most comprehensive packages. A 2007 escribe helpline add-on was offered at £200 for the first year as an introductory price. No current escribe price appeared in the search results. — [CAT Magazine](https://www.catmag.co.uk/news/escribes-monthly-pay-plans-hope-to-suit-all-budgets); [AM Online 2007](https://am-online.com/news/2007/11/16/thatcham-to-offer-direct-access-to-its-crash-repair-data-library/16051)
- In 2008, Thatcham said licence fees to access all the manufacturers' technical data came to £36,000 in total, "a cost which few bodyshops could bear". — [AM Online 2008, via search summary](https://www.am-online.com/news/2008/7/23/bodyshop-news-thatcham-s-repair-methods-call/17827/)
- **ADAS:** Since 2017, escribe has shown ADAS fitment (AEB, reverse AEB, lane assist, park assist, and whether each is standard or optional fit) so that estimates can be adjusted. Thatcham's position is that if ADAS sensors, or parts close to them, are in the repair specification, they must be calibrated after the repair to VM tolerances. — [Thatcham ADAS fitment release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/thatcham-research-the-first-to-deliver-adas-fitment-features-to-repairers-fingertips-1934580); [Thatcham ADAS calibration release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/thatcham-research/pressreleases/adas-post-repair-calibration-to-vehicle-manufacturer-tolerances-is-essential-2870958)

### Inferences
- The term "Thatcham times" in UK estimates today mostly means MET or Thatcham panel times embedded in an estimating system (Audatex being the documented case), or the methods content in escribe. It does not mean a standalone feed that can be bought.
- Licensing Thatcham data into a new estimating application would almost certainly be a bespoke B2B negotiation, comparable to the Audatex, Suncorp or Mitchell deals. It is not self-serve. The 2006 audit condition suggests Thatcham controls quality and use of its times within licensee systems.
- Thatcham repair *methods* are not repair *times*. Licensing escribe methods alone would not supply labour hours.

### Gaps
- No public information on current terms, fees or API for licensing Thatcham MET times or paint data to an independent developer. Thatcham must be contacted directly. thatcham.org could not be fetched.
- Nothing found on products named "Thatcham Methods Assistant", "Thatcham Paint Times" or a "Thatcham paint materials index". They may not exist under those names. I found no source describing a separate Thatcham paint-times methodology. UK paint calculation in the systems I could document is AZT-based (see section 5).
- Whether Thatcham still maintains MET labour times for current models, as opposed to methods only, is not confirmed.
- No Thatcham-published ADAS calibration *times*.

## 2. Audatex (Solera), GT Motive and Glass's / J.D. Power Repair Estimate: which time sources they embed, and whether third parties can use them through APIs

### Takeaway
Three commercial UK estimating systems embed licensed labour and paint times:
- **Audatex** uses mainly OEM times, with Thatcham panel times for some marques and its own additions, plus AZT paint.
- **GT Motive** uses OE-based data and AZT paint.
- **Glass's / J.D. Power Repair Estimate** uses data supplied under contract by more than 40 manufacturers, plus AZT paint.

None publishes a self-serve developer API that would let a third party pull raw labour times into its own estimator. The integration products that do exist (AudaConnect, AudaBridge, Qapter APIs, GT Motive partner integrations) connect *with* the vendor's estimate rather than handing over its times. No subscription prices are currently published.

### Cited Findings
- **Audatex data sourcing** (from Audatex's Australian site, which may not match UK practice): vehicle manufacturers supply methods and times directly to Audatex's international data centres. For some marques (Jaguar, Land Rover and Rover), Thatcham panel times are used. Where an OEM publishes no times, Audatex relies on independent research bodies (Jikken, AZT, Samsung Fire & Marine, KART). Where an OEM gives no time for a specific remove/refit or replacement operation, Audatex may add one. — [Audatex Australia](https://audatex.com.au/?p=154)
- Audatex told the Competition Commission (2013–14) that estimating systems use data from manufacturers' manuals and Thatcham repair standards to set the parts, paint quantity and labour time for each job. — [CC working paper, "Horizontal concentration in repair cost estimation systems", 2013 (search summary)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5329ded840f0b60a760002b2/130729_toh_3_horizontal_concentration_in_repair_cost_estimation_systems.pdf)
- **UK market structure (2013, historical):** the main UK systems were Audatex and Glassmatix, plus a third from Inter-est. Nine of the ten largest insurers recommended or required Audatex across their repair networks. Repairers said Audatex was more expensive than alternatives, but many said they would still choose it. — [CC working paper 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5329ded840f0b60a760002b2/130729_toh_3_horizontal_concentration_in_repair_cost_estimation_systems.pdf)
- **Audatex paint:** Audatex paint and materials figures come from the AZT paint calculation system. Audatex UK periodically updates its paint-material price tables, for example on 26 March 2024 and 23 April 2025, based on a "basket of paint brands used in our system". — [Autobiz.ie, "Don't be deceived on paint discount"](https://autobiz.ie/home/dont-be-deceived-on-paint-discount); [Audatex UK paint update March 2024](https://audatex.co.uk/uk-paint-update-25-march-2024/); [Audatex UK data update April 2025](https://audatex.co.uk/audatex-uk-data-update-23rd-april-2025/)
- AudaEnterpriseGold 4.0 added new-part painting for plastics. The user picks the delivery condition of the part (LE, LE2, LE3, LE4), and that choice changes the paint labour and materials generated. — [Audatex UK AEG 4.0 feature overview](https://audatex.co.uk/audaenterprisegold-4-0-feature-overview-plastic-new-part-painting￼/)
- **Audatex / Solera integration products:** AudaConnect is described as an encrypted, API-based integration platform. AudaBridge is a hosted service that connects back-office and third-party systems to Solera's central data repository. Qapter Guided Image Capture and Qapter Intelligent Triage are API products. Qapter estimating integrates with AudaEnterpriseGold. Integration partners include AutoFlow, with a two-way repair-status feed. No public developer portal or terms were found. — [Audatex UK solutions](https://audatex.co.uk/solutions/); [AudaBridge](https://audatex.co.uk/solutions/audabridge/); [AutoFlow collaboration](https://audatex.co.uk/solera-audatex-and-autoflow-strengthen-long-term-collaboration-to-further-improve-repair-estimating-efficiency)
- **Audatex pricing:** Insurance Times reported a pay-as-you-go AudaEnterprise option for UK bodyshops with fewer than six assessments a month: £28 transaction fee per assessment plus the standard assessment charge. The article is undated and probably old. NBRA reported Audatex price rises of 3.5% (around 2018). No current AEG price was found. — [Insurance Times](https://www.insurancetimes.co.uk/audatex-offers-pay-as-you-go-service/1327863.article); [Collision Week 2018](https://collisionweek.com/2018/06/08/uk-national-body-repair-association-calls-insurers-address-collision-repair-cost-inflation)
- **GT Motive:** GT Estimate offers parts databases, labour times and vehicle graphics. Verisk (2022) says it gives access to manufacturer data including VIN, ADAS and service-maintenance-repair information. GT Motive's UK material says its data is based on OE data and AZT paint, built to UK standards. It entered the UK around 2020 through DAC Holdings, works with Vizion Network and NFU Mutual, and is an NBRA Partner Member. — [GT Estimate guide](https://marketing.gtmotive.com/external/gt_estimate_complete_guide.pdf); [Verisk](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/verisk-announces-european-collaboration-with-gt-motive-to-expedite-motor-damage-claims/); [GT Motive UK teaser 2020](https://marketing.gtmotive.com/external/files/EN/gt-global-claims-uk-teaser-2020.pdf); [Bodyshop Magazine, 500th sign-up](https://www.bodyshopmag.com/?p=40464); [Bodyshop Magazine, NBRA partner](https://www.bodyshopmag.com/?p=57199)
- **GT Motive third-party integrations:** Carform exchanges technical data, labour times, parts references and prices with GT Motive automatically. PlanSo (Germany, 2023) gives its customers direct access to GT Estimate. Partly also lists a GT Motive integration. In each case the integration runs through a partnership, and no public API terms were found. — [Carform x GT Motive](https://carform.io/?p=6048); [Autohaus, PlanSo](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/schadenbusiness/digitale-auftragsabwicklung-planso-und-kalkulationsdienstleister-gt-motive-kooperieren-3442434); [Partly](https://www.partly.com/integrations/gt-motive)
- GT Motive "rules out price increases in 2026" (headline only; no figures seen). — [Bodyshop Magazine](https://www.bodyshopmag.com/?p=56123)
- **Glass's / J.D. Power Repair Estimate:** it replaced Glassmatix in 2017. It uses data supplied under contract by more than 40 manufacturers, parts prices drawn directly from manufacturer databases, and AZT-sourced paint data. Some marques supply no data (for example Ferrari and Bentley). No price was found. — [Fleet News 2017](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/fleet-industry-news/2017/04/25/glass-s-replaces-glassmatix-with-new-repair-estimating-system); [Glass's Repair Estimate support](https://glass.co.uk/repair-estimate-product-support/); [J.D. Power Repair Estimate](https://www.jdpower.com/business/repair-estimate/)
- **ADAS operations:** Solera's Qapter has an "ADAS Operations" estimating module that lists calibration procedures and labour times separately from the repair estimate. This is mainly US material. In the US, CCC's Azimuth combines OEM procedures, build data and time studies to identify calibrations and their labour times. — [search summary, Repairer Driven News / BodyShop Business](https://www.bodyshopbusiness.com/ccc-intelligent-solutions-adds-azimuth-to-diagnostics-network/)

### Inferences
- The realistic legal routes are:
  - integrate with Audatex, GT Motive or Repair Estimate as a partner, so that the user holds a licence and the estimate is produced in or by that system; or
  - license an estimating engine or white-label data under a commercial agreement.
  
  Extracting their times into your own database would breach their licences.
- Because Audatex dominates UK insurer networks (as of 2013, and very likely still), estimates that are not on Audatex or another recognised system face acceptance friction with insurers.

### Gaps
- No public UK API documentation, partner-programme terms or prices for AudaConnect, AudaBridge, Qapter or GT Motive.
- No UK-specific statement of Audatex's labour-time source hierarchy (OEM versus Thatcham versus Audatex-added). The Australian page is the best proxy available.
- Current UK market shares are not found.

## 3. Autodata and HaynesPro (Infopro / TecAlliance-type providers): do their labour times cover collision operations?

### Takeaway
Autodata and HaynesPro labour times are aimed at mechanical, service and repair work. No source showed collision or body-panel operations (panel replace, R&I for body repair, repair levels, refinish) in their labour-time products. Both offer data APIs and web services for integration, but API pricing is not published. ALLDATA Labour Times (an AutoZone company, with data from TecAlliance) is the only aftermarket product found that claims both mechanical and body labour times.

### Cited Findings

- A 2026 article from a competitor's blog says Autodata publishes OEM-derived operation times for mechanical areas (engine, brakes, HVAC) and that full collision estimating uses different tools. This is a promotional source with low weight. — [workshopdata.us blog (competitor; low reliability)](https://www.workshopdata.us/he/blog/autodata-ai-labour-times-pricing-2026)

- HaynesPro's labour-time module adds associated tasks to official times and includes overlap calculations and standard times linked to generic parts. HaynesPro Web Services lets maintenance, repair and diagnostic data be built into third-party online applications. — [HaynesPro WorkshopData](https://www.haynespro.com/workshops); [Infopro Digital Automotive, HaynesPro](https://www.infopro-digital-automotive.com/uk/haynespro-data/)
- Reseller pricing for HaynesPro is unofficial and conflicting: "from £59/month or £517/year" on one UK reseller, and "£35–£50 a month" for a single user estimated on a competing reseller's comparison blog. - ALLDATA Labour Times supplies OEM labour times for mechanical and body repairs, with data from TecAlliance. Tekmetric integrates ALLDATA's labour guide through an API, which requires an ALLDATA Repair or Collision subscription. This is a US-centred source. — [ALLDATA EU Labour Times](https://www.alldata.com/eu/en/alldata-labour-times); [ALLDATA press release](https://alldata.com/us/alldata-launches-alldata-labour-times); [Tekmetric](https://tours.tekmetric.com/answers/integrate-alldata-with-tekmetric)

### Inferences
- Autodata and HaynesPro could support mechanical lines on a collision estimate (for example radiator, condenser or suspension work after an impact). They should not be relied on for body-panel times, repair levels or paint.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation from Autodata or HaynesPro on whether any body or collision operations are included. Ask the vendors directly.
- No official API pricing for Autodata, HaynesPro or ALLDATA EU.
- I could not verify whether the workshopdata.pro / workshopdata.us sites are authorised HaynesPro resellers.

## 4. Manufacturer repair times (OEM labour operation codes): access for independents under UK RMI rules

### Takeaway
Under the retained UK version of Regulation (EC) 715/2007 and successor type-approval rules, OEMs must give independent operators non-discriminatory access to RMI, for reasonable fees with hourly, daily, monthly and yearly options. Some OEMs do expose labour times this way: Mercedes-Benz has offered XENTRY Operation Time (XOT) to independents since April 2022, and VW Group publishes repair information through erWin. Access is per workshop, per user or per VIN, for repair purposes. Nothing found suggests it permits bulk extraction or redistribution in third-party estimating software. That right is what estimating houses obtain through contracts with OEMs.

### Cited Findings
- Reg. 715/2007 Article 6 (retained in UK law) requires manufacturers to give independent operators unrestricted, standardised access to RMI through websites on a non-discriminatory basis. Fees must be "reasonable and proportionate". The 2018/858 framework requires hourly, daily, monthly and yearly access options. — [legislation.gov.uk, Reg 715/2007 Ch. III](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2007/715/chapter/III/2020-01-31); [Reg 2018/858 Ch. XIV](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/858/chapter/XIV/adopted)
- UK AFCAR input to the block-exemption review said RMI is accessible, but not to the extent envisioned when the regulation was introduced. The UK Motor Vehicle Block Exemption Order took effect on 1 June 2023. — [UK AFCAR submission (gov.uk)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6182516ae90e07197d8fb949/Evaluation_of_Motor_Vehicle_Block_Exemption_Regulations-Initial_Input_from_UK_AFCAR-_Final_draft_2.pdf); [DAC Beachcroft](https://www.dacbeachcroft.com/en/what-we-think/new-order-extends-competition-in-motor-repair-market)
- LKQ, an advocacy source, argues that the UK lacks a formal access scheme comparable to the EU's and risks falling behind. — [LKQ Europe](https://lkqeurope.com/article/public-affairs/europe-moving-forward-rightful-access-all-repair-and-maintenance-information)
- **Mercedes-Benz:** independent workshops and ISPs have been able to use XENTRY WIS and XENTRY Operation Time (XOT) since 11 April 2022. XOT gives VIN-filtered operation items and times, updated daily. The XENTRY Combo Package gives 24-hour access to WIS, XOT and Tips, using workshop-wide credits and user-specific VIN activations, and requires MFA. Use is governed by Mercedes-Benz UK general terms for applications and data content (dated 06/2025). No UK price list was found. — [B2B Connect GB, Combo Package](https://b2bconnect.mercedes-benz.com/gb/help/faq/software/xentry-combo-package); [Mercedes-Benz UK GTC Applications Data Content 06/2025](https://uk.xentry-shop.mercedes-benz.com/out/pictures/ddmedia/UK_GTC_Applications_Data_Content_EN_06_2025.pdf)
- **VW Group:** erWin gives independent workshops paid access to OEM repair, maintenance and diagnostic information, including bodywork repair groups. Access can be bought for one hour, one day or at a flat rate, and UK registration needs a company registration number. No erWin labour-time product was confirmed in the results. — [Volkswagen UK independent repairers](https://www.volkswagen.co.uk/en/need-help/partnering-with-volkswagen/technical-form.html); [Volkswagen Ireland](https://www.volkswagen.ie/en/owners-and-users.html)
- Glass's Repair Estimate says its times come from data "contractually provided by over 40 manufacturers". This shows that redistribution in estimating software rests on separate OEM data contracts. — [Glass's Repair Estimate](https://glass.co.uk/repair-estimate-product-support/)

### Inferences
- An independent app *user* (a bodyshop) can legally consult OEM times per VIN on the OEM portal and type them into an estimate. A *developer* cannot lawfully scrape or bulk-load OEM portal data into a product without a separate data-licence agreement with each OEM. The Mercedes GTCs should be read on this point. Building on OEM data means 30+ separate negotiations, which is what Audatex, GT Motive and Glass's already hold.

### Gaps
- The exact redistribution or derivative-use clauses in the Mercedes UK GTCs and erWin terms were not read, because the PDF could not be fetched.
- No UK XOT or erWin prices were found ("not published" in search results).
- Not checked: BMW (AOS), Stellantis, Toyota TIS, Ford.

## 5. UK estimate conventions: operation types, repair and paint levels, blend, paint materials

### Takeaway
UK estimates produced by Audatex, GT Motive and Repair Estimate use:
- OEM-style labour operations: replace (renew), remove & refit / R&I, and repair hours that the assessor or estimator enters;
- AZT paint calculation, the de facto European standard, for paint labour and materials. AZT stages for metal are surface, repair under 50%, repair over 50% and new part. For plastic they are surface, repair under 15%, new part primed and new part unprimed;
- paint materials *calculated* by AZT from area and paint type, priced from the system's paint tables, often with insurer discounts.

Blend appears to be treated as part of the paint operation (adjacent-panel blending is often part of the refinish time) and is a frequent dispute point. I found no published UK Audatex blend percentage.

### Cited Findings
- AZT describes its paint calculation as a manufacturer-independent standard used in more than 35 countries and available through the calculation software of Solera-Audatex, Autovista-Eurotax, DAT, GT Motive and CAB, among others. — [AZT paint calculation system](https://www.azt-automotive.com/en/products-services/AZT-paint-calculation-system)
- AZT is a calculation aid that records repair-specific and model-specific values. Working times start from the paintable surface. It defines a wide range of paint stages for new plastic parts based on delivery condition, with "K"-prefixed codes. A comprehensive AZT update was being piloted as of March 2025. — [AZT system description 2020](https://www.azt-automotive.com/_Resources/Persistent/b70048078929e9cc459f872d1f3bcab6a62cf07d/AZT%20Paint%20Calculation_System%20Description_2020-10-27.pdf); [AZT plastics usage PDF](https://www.azt-automotive.com/_Resources/Persistent/3fb1db1302aae434d93ab2ce42a8a5e6c9dce2cb/AZT%20Paint%20Calculation%20Usage%20for%20Repair%20Painting%20of%20Plastic%20Components.pdf); [AZT March 2025 update](https://www.azt-automotive.com/en/topics/Update-AZT-Paint-Calculation-System-1)
- Paint levels as listed in DAT and AZT-based documents. These are German or DAT sources and may differ in detail from the Audatex UK definitions.
  - Metal: surface, repair under 50%, repair over 50%, new part.
  - Plastic: surface, repair under 15%, new part prepared, new part unprepared.
  - "Surface" means cleaning and scuffing the existing topcoat and applying a new topcoat.
  - DAT maps these to levels 1 to 4 (level 1 new part, level 2 surface/topcoat, level 3 repair under 50%, level 4 repair over 50%).
  
  — [DAT LIF 00-060](https://www.dat.de/sphinx/help/printInfos/pdf/de/LIF_00-060_DE_EN.pdf); [DAT LIF 00-EUL](https://www.dat.de/sphinx/help/printInfos/pdf/de/LIF_00-EUL_DE_EN.pdf)
- DAT systems use the manufacturer paint system where one exists, otherwise AZT or DAT-Eurolack. — [SilverDAT CalculatePro description](https://dynamic-www.dat.de/fileadmin/de/download/rechtliches/produktbeschreibung-silverdat-calculatepro-english-version.pdf)
- Audatex UK paint notices say the per-job effect of price changes depends on the paint operation (new part, surface, repair), the paint type and the surface area. — [Audatex UK paint update Feb 2023](https://audatex.co.uk/uk-paint-update-28th-february-2023/)
- Precedence and blend examples from OEM guidance found in AZT and DAT search results:
  - A Mazda document ranks repair paintwork above new-part paintwork, and new-part above surface paintwork.
  - BMW guidance says its labour-time units include blending the paint material.
  - Mazda includes blending into the topcoat up to about a hand's width.

- **Blend (US comparators only):**
  - US systems historically used 50% of refinish time for a two-stage blend; CCC and Mitchell used 70% for three-stage.
  - CCC and Solera later changed their guidance to say blend time must be assessed vehicle by vehicle.
  - Mitchell keeps 50% but now lets users set their own percentage.
  - An SCRS study found blending took on average 31.59% *more* time than a full refinish.

Other decisions mention blending into adjacent panels for colour match (DRN-5898191), and an estimate stating that at most one quarter panel needed blending (DRN-5614689). — [FOS DRN-5913560](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5913560.pdf); [FOS DRN-5898191](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5898191.pdf); [FOS DRN-5614689](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5614689.pdf)

  - Calculated by the system (AZT) rather than charged as a flat rate per hour.
  - Many insurers take up to a 25% discount off calculated paint and materials. Without a network agreement, repairers have no obligation to give one.
  - Network agreements cited include a 20% paint discount (anecdotal).

- **Labour-time conventions:** published times assume new, undamaged parts. Hidden damage and access work add time. ### Inferences
- An app that wants insurer-compatible output should model operations the way Audatex and AZT do:
  - replace, R&I and repair hours (repair is the estimator's judgement, often with a few typical bands);
  - paint stage per panel (new part / surface / repair under or over a threshold; plastics by delivery condition);
  - materials calculated by area, paint type and stage, not a flat rate per hour.
  
  Reproducing AZT values without a licence is not lawful. Cloning the *structure* of the stages is probably fine, but this needs legal advice.
- No evidence was found of a "Thatcham paint materials index" used in UK estimates. Materials appear to come from the AZT-based calculation in each system, priced from the vendor's paint-price tables.

- No current UK paint-materials rate per refinish hour found.
- AZT licensing terms or fees for a new software vendor: not published.

## 6. Open or low-cost alternatives, ADAS calibration times, and the risks of building your own guide times

### Takeaway
I found no open or free dataset of UK collision labour or paint times. The low-cost legal options are:
- let users enter their own times or OEM-portal times manually;
- partner with Audatex, GT Motive or Repair Estimate so that the user's licence produces the times;
- license AZT for paint and an OEM-data aggregator (for example TecAlliance through ALLDATA EU) for some R&I and replace times.

Self-built guide times carry real acceptance risk. UK insurers overwhelmingly specify Audatex, insurer engineers challenge non-system estimates, and FOS judges outcomes against "suitably qualified motor engineer" assessments.

### Cited Findings
- In 2013, nine of the ten largest insurers recommended or required Audatex for their networks, and repairers said they were *required* by work providers to use it. — [CC working paper 2013](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5329ded840f0b60a760002b2/130729_toh_3_horizontal_concentration_in_repair_cost_estimation_systems.pdf)
- Even Audatex, the incumbent, faced accusations from the Body Repair Industry Campaign of underestimating repair times by up to 10% (older). This shows that guide times are contested even when they come from recognised systems. — [Post Online](https://www.postonline.co.uk/post/news/1217754/audatex-accusations-poor-repair-estimates)
- FOS treats an estimate or offer from a suitably qualified motor engineer as evidence of fair settlement. Consumers must prove that a different method or time would give a better outcome. — [FOS DRN-5913560](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5913560.pdf)
- No Thatcham or estimating-system ADAS calibration times were found. The only UK figures came from a vendor blog with no methodology: static single forward-camera calibration at £80–£180 and 45–90 minutes. BookMyGarage says calibration jobs take 30 minutes to a few hours. Insurance Times cites a Thatcham-linked example in which an ADAS Ford Focus windscreen replacement cost rose 123% once calibration was included. — [workshopdata.us blog (low reliability)](https://www.workshopdata.us/en/blog/adas-calibration-cost-uk); [BookMyGarage](https://bookmygarage.com/repairs/adas-calibration/); [Insurance Times](https://www.insurancetimes.co.uk/urgent-action-needed-as-vehicle-repair-costs-soar-32-thatcham-/1421834.article)
- Thatcham and insurers set Insurance Industry Requirements for ADAS repair in 2020: calibration to VM tolerances and evidence of it. — [Fleet News 2020](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/car-industry-news/2020/07/31/insurance-industry-requirements-established-for-adas-equipped-vehicles); [Fleet News 2019](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/car-industry-news/2019/05/09/thatcham-outlines-adas-re-alignment-guidance)
- US data shows the scale of missed calibrations: a Canadian study found 88% of required ADAS calibrations missed. Its methodology is disputed. — [Repairer Driven News 2020](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2020/10/28/study-finds-canadian-body-shops-appraisal-firms-missed-88-of-required-adas-calibrations/)

### Inferences
- **Legal options, from cheapest to most expensive:**
  1. User-entered or OEM-portal times per VIN, with the app as a calculator. This is cheap and legal, but results are not "recognised".
  2. An integration partnership with Audatex, GT Motive or Repair Estimate. The user pays the vendor, and the app consumes or exports estimates. Recognition is high.
  3. A direct data licence with Thatcham, AZT and OEMs. This is bespoke, likely costs six figures or more (not published), and is slow.
- **Risks of self-built guide times:** insurer engineers reject or cut them, they carry no audit pedigree (Thatcham historically withheld data from systems that failed audit), consumers and garages can challenge them at FOS or small claims, and copying times from licensed systems or OEM portals raises IP and database-right exposure.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of any open-licence UK collision times dataset.
- No published price for licensing AZT, Thatcham or OEM data feeds to software developers.
- No UK ADAS calibration labour-time standard from Thatcham or the estimating systems.
